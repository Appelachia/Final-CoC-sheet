🎲 Randomize Investigator · CoC 7e Character Creation

Roll your eight characteristics and Luck, then distribute all occupation points to the appropriate eight professional skills plus Credit Rating, and all personal-interest points to four other skills. Other skills retain their printed base chances.

Choose exactly eight occupation skills

Credit Rating is funded separately from the same occupation point pool. Name any blank/custom skill on your sheet first if you want to select it here.

Age 15–89 applies the appropriate 7e EDU, Luck, physical-characteristic, and Move adjustments automatically. Characters outside that range need Keeper approval. Untick “Create a new sheet” to replace the active sheet's stats and skills; its name, portrait, notes and roll history will be preserved.

Choose an occupation and age, then generate. Occupation points follow EDU × 4 or EDU × 2 + the higher of STR/DEX × 2, as appropriate. Personal-interest points equal INT × 2.
Portrait:
You can also tap the blank portrait. Uploaded images are compressed for saving.

Use the Dice Roller alongside the sheet for CoC percentile checks, bonus/penalty dice, and damage rolls. Tap a number in HP, Sanity, Luck, or Magic Points to move that section’s highlighted value. Printed skill names and original base percentages are fixed; change the Full score or use a blank custom skill slot. Save Sheet stores records on this browser, and Download Backup keeps a transferable copy.
